Learn how to use SmartFarmAI effectively

Welcome to SmartFarmAI

This application uses Machine Learning to provide recommendations and predictions based on environmental and soil conditions.

1. Crop Recommendation

Enter the local temperature, humidity, and soil moisture levels. Our ML model will recommend the most suitable crop to plant under these conditions.

2. Disease Detection

Upload an image of a crop leaf. The deep learning CNN model will analyze the leaf for signs of common diseases and provide a diagnosis along with treatment recommendations.

3. Fertilizer Guide

Provide your soil's Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium (NPK) levels and pH. The system will recommend the appropriate type of fertilizer.

4. Weather Prediction

A dedicated ML model predicts local weather conditions based on your area's current environmental parameters.
